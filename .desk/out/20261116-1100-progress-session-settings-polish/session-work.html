Bodyweight squat

12reps

Stop with 3 reps left in reserve

A fundamental two-leg squat that builds strength in the quadriceps and glutes and grooves a sound squat pattern.

Pay attention to

  • Feet under hips
  • Knees track toes
  • Stand tall at the top
How to
  1. Stand with your feet about hip- to shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly.
  2. Bend your hips and knees together to sit down between your heels.
  3. Lower as deep as you can while keeping your chest up and heels on the floor.
  4. Push through your whole foot to stand tall at the top.
