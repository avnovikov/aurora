Glute bridge

Lower body · Glutes & hips · Beginner

Muscles: glutes (primary)

Personal best

No personal best yet — log a clean set.

A floor-based hip lift that strengthens the glutes while keeping the ribs down and the spine neutral.

How to

  1. Lie on your back with your knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart.
  2. Brace lightly and press through your heels to lift your hips.
  3. Rise until there is a straight line from knees to shoulders and squeeze your glutes.
  4. Lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

Cues

  • Ribs down
  • Squeeze at the top
  • No overarching

Common mistakes

  • Arching the lower back at the top
  • Pushing through the toes instead of the heels
  • Knees falling in or out
