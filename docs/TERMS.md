## 1. What Aurora is

Aurora is private calisthenics training software. You set who you are and what to train; the app keeps a plan queue and picks exercise and dose from sleep, pulse, load, missed reps, and stalls. It is personal software, not a clinical or medical product.

## 2. Privacy and data

In version 0.1, training and health-related data stay **on your device**. There is no cloud account and no server sync of that data. Full detail — including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA framing — is in [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md).

## 3. Not medical advice

Aurora does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease. Exercise and dose suggestions are rule-based coaching aids. If you have a medical condition, injury, or doubt about training, consult a qualified clinician. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or distress.
