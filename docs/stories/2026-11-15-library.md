# Story: Library list redesign

As someone choosing a move one-handed on my phone,
I want a calm, compact exercise list with search and filters within thumb reach,
so that I can scan many moves quickly and narrow them without stretching to the top of the screen.

Design source: `.desk/out/20261115-2201-library-screen-redesign/final.html` (signed off).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Header: "Library" eyebrow, "Exercises" title, and the result count ("469 exercises") on the title line, with the number as the screen's one accent figure.
- [ ] Rows are compact scan lines: the name in bold, a meta line (area · muscle · level · gear) with the level emphasised, then the one-line description. Target / joint / Excluded badges stay.
- [ ] Search, the hidden-stretch hint, Clear filters and the three filter panels sit in a dock at the bottom, just above the tab bar, and stay there while the list scrolls.
- [ ] Each panel's trigger shows its name and current value: "Show: Hide stretches", "Area & muscle: All areas · All muscles", "Level: All levels" (and the chosen values when filtered).
- [ ] Opening a panel shows its chips in a sheet above the dock; one panel is open at a time.
- [ ] Search, filtering, filter state, counts, Clear filters and row selection (opens the detail page) behave as before; `data-search`, `data-panel`, `data-exercise-id`, chip and toggle `data-*` hooks are unchanged.
- [ ] Colours come only from the role variables; works in all six palettes. Touch targets are at least 44px.

## Out of scope
- The exercise detail page (exercise story).
- The mock's expanded-row doodle peek and its palette / doodle mock controls (preview-only).
- The tab bar itself.
