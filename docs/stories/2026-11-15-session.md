# Story: Session screen, dose as hero

As someone mid-workout with the phone propped up an arm's length away,
I want the in-workout screen to show the time, where I am, the move and its dose at a glance,
so that I can read what to do next without picking the phone up or hunting for it.

Design source: `.desk/out/20261115-2200-session-doodle-frame-review/final.html` (signed off),
with `20261115-2140-session-screen-redesign/final-plex.html` for the rest of the layout.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Header: "Elapsed 0:01" and "Left 31:40" side by side, the numbers in the display font; both tick every second as before (`[data-session-clock]`).
- [ ] A segmented progress bar for the stations (warm-up, each work slot, cool-down) replaces the dot row; the current station is wider and marked `aria-current="step"`, each segment names its station for screen readers.
- [ ] Under the bar, the small line with phase, minutes, move and next move (`chalkNowLabel`, unchanged).
- [ ] Hero: phase eyebrow, move name in the display font, then the dose as a very large number in the accent colour with its unit in muted ("59 s" on a station, "8 reps" or "30 s" on a work set, the rest countdown in "s").
- [ ] The doodle sits below the dose in its own full-width row; its markup and `.doodle` styles are unchanged.
- [ ] Then the description, "Pay attention to" cues and the citation, in that order.
- [ ] A bar pinned to the bottom holds "Pulse —" and the Strap control; opening Strap shows Pulse ok / Pulse lost (or Connect pulse / Disconnect on the phone) above the bar. A lost pulse shows in the alert colour.
- [ ] Every timer, swipe, Log set / Failed / Skip, rest, Get ready, warm-up, side-switch, cool-down and pulse behaviour and every `data-*` hook works as before.
- [ ] Doses are rendered from the existing data only; nothing is computed, rounded or changed.
- [ ] Colours only through the role variables; works in all six palettes; 4px grid; 44px touch targets.
- [ ] `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit` pass.

## Out of scope

- The doodles themselves (`src/doodles/`, `public/doodles/`, `doodleHtml`, `.doodle` CSS).
- Copy changes suggested in the design review ("Pulse OK", "Warm-up" spelling).
- A visible pause control for doodle motion.
- Today, Library, Exercise detail and font bundling (other stories).
