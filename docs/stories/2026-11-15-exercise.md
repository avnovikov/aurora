# Story: Exercise detail redesign

As someone browsing the Library before a morning session,
I want the exercise page to read top to bottom like a coaching card (what it is, how I am doing, how to do it, where I stand, what to do next),
so that I can learn a move and act on it without hunting through a mixed list of blocks.

Source: signed-off mock `.desk/out/20261115-2205-exercise-screen-redesign/final.html` (design worktree). Preview-only parts (DEV bar, palette and doodle pickers) are left out.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reading order follows the mock: ← Back to list; Exercise eyebrow, name in the display font, area · muscle · level line, Muscles line; Personal best; media row (doodle or photos); description; How to; Cues; Common mistakes; Standards; Your level select; Plan actions with hint; Progression; Your sessions (Not for me) with hint; text source line.
- [ ] Safety, joint-care and joint-load blocks, when present, sit after the description and before How to (they are not in the mock; they keep their markup).
- [ ] The doodle `<img class="doodle">` markup and its CSS are unchanged; it only moves into its own full-width row under the personal best. Photos (licensed images) use the same row.
- [ ] How to is a numbered list with large muted numerals in the display font.
- [ ] Cues and Common mistakes are ruled lists (edge lines between items), no bullets.
- [ ] Standards are a full-bleed scale of five equal columns (Novice … Elite, numbers as today, holds keep their "s"); the user's level (the same tier the select shows) is highlighted with a top bar, surface fill and a "Your level" note.
- [ ] Your level has a visible label tied to the select; the select keeps `data-skill-tier`, its accessible name, and saves on change. Its hint and the cascade hint are unchanged.
- [ ] Plan actions: two equal buttons, Generate plan as the only accent (filled) action, Add to the plan outlined; the hint and plan status line follow.
- [ ] Progression links are full-width stacked buttons (Easier · …, Harder · …) and still open that exercise.
- [ ] Not for me is a full-width tick-box button; it keeps `data-exclude-toggle`, `data-kind="toggle"`, `aria-pressed` and the exclude/restore behaviour; the hint changes with it as today.
- [ ] Every label, number, action, data-* hook and the Add to the plan day picker flow are unchanged.
- [ ] Colours only through the role variables; fonts through `--font-body` / `--font-display` with fallbacks; 4px grid; controls at least 44px tall. Works in all six palettes.
- [ ] Styles live in `src/styles/exercise.css`, imported from `src/library/libraryDetail.ts`.

## Out of scope

- Doodles (images, helpers, `.doodle` CSS).
- The Library list, the plan day picker screen layout, the tab bar and the shared shell.
- Any change to doses, standards or level logic.
