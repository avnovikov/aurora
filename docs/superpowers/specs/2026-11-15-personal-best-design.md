# Personal best — design

**Status:** approved for implementation (2026-11-15)  
**Issue:** #41

## Intent

Motivational glance at stored `bestReps` (clean logged set), not a stats suite.

## Surfaces

1. **Library detail** — brass **Personal best** hero under the exercise title. Empty: *No personal best yet — log a clean set.* Drop the quiet “Best logged” hint under Your level.
2. **Progress** — on plan-target path cards only, under **Now / suggested start**: compact `PB · 12 reps` (or `s`) when that step has `bestReps`.

## Out of scope

Manual edit, charts, Library list chips.
